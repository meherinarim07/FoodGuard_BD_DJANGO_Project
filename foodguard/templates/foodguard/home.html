How it works

From complaint to action in four steps

Every report follows the same transparent journey, so nothing gets lost.

1

Report

Choose the vendor, describe the problem and upload a photo as evidence.

2

Verify

Independent verifiers review the evidence and give an honest verdict.

3

Protect

Fraud is flagged, vendor risk is scored and area alerts go out automatically.

4

Resolve

Authorities investigate, take action and upload proof that it was fixed.

Who uses FoodGuard

One platform, three roles

Everyone has a clear job, which keeps the system fair and trustworthy.

Citizens

Anyone can speak up when food is unsafe.

  • Report a vendor and food item
  • Upload photo evidence
  • Track report status and trust score

Verifiers

Trusted reviewers who check the facts.

  • Review pending reports and evidence
  • Rate confidence and evidence quality
  • Two opinions decide the result

Authorities

Officers who take real action.

  • Investigate verified reports
  • Upload proof of resolution
  • Handle fraud flags and area alerts
